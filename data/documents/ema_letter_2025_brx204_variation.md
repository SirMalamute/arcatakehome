# EMA — Type II Variation Request for Supplementary Information

**Date:** 9 April 2025
**Procedure:** EMEA/H/C/005640/II/0014
**Product:** BRX-204 (orvatimab) solution for injection in pre-filled pen
**MAH:** Borealis Therapeutics Europe B.V.

The variation proposes extending the shelf life of the pre-filled pen from 24 to 36 months at 2–8°C.

## Request for Supplementary Information

1. Provide real-time stability data up to 36 months for at least three commercial-scale batches. Only two batches with 36-month data were submitted.
2. Confirm whether any out-of-trend results were observed for high molecular weight species (SEC-HPLC) beyond 24 months.
3. The proposed acceptance criterion for subvisible particles (≥10 µm: ≤6000 per container) should be justified with reference to Ph. Eur. 2.9.19.

The MAH committed to provide the third-batch 36-month data by 30 September 2025. The variation will be held in clock-stop until then. Owner: Jonas Weber, EU Regulatory Lead.
