# EMA — Day 120 List of Questions (Quality Excerpt)

**Date:** 15 November 2022
**Procedure:** EMEA/H/C/005812
**Product:** BRX-330 (salvenidol) inhalation powder, 100 micrograms
**Applicant:** Borealis Therapeutics Europe B.V.

## Major Objections

None in the quality area.

## Other Concerns — Drug Product

**Q14.** The applicant is asked to justify the absence of a delivered dose uniformity test across the device life (beginning, middle and end of device) in the shelf-life specification.

**Q15.** Stability data at the intermediate condition (30°C/65% RH) are provided for only two of three primary batches. The applicant should provide data on the third batch or justify the omission.

**Q16.** The applicant should confirm that the lactose monohydrate supplier complies with the TSE/BSE requirements of Ph. Eur. 5.2.8 and provide the relevant certificate.

The applicant committed in the clock-stop request to submit responses to Q14–Q16 with the Day 121 responses, by 20 March 2023. Responses will be coordinated by the EU Regulatory Lead, Jonas Weber.
