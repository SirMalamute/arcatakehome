# Health Canada — Acknowledgement of Annual Drug Notification

**Date:** 11 June 2024
**To:** Borealis Therapeutics Inc.
**Product:** BRX-101 (velantrine hydrochloride) 50 mg film-coated tablets

Health Canada acknowledges receipt of the Annual Drug Notification dated 30 April 2024.

The Directorate confirms that the 24-month and 36-month long-term stability data for Lots BX1-0012, BX1-0013 and BX1-0014 were received on 28 March 2023, fulfilling the stability commitment made in the letter of 14 September 2021. The 36-month shelf life is accepted.

The commitment to tighten the dissolution acceptance criterion to Q = 80% at 30 minutes remains open. Only 7 commercial batches have been manufactured to date. Please provide an update in the next Annual Drug Notification, due by 30 April 2025.

Health Canada
Bureau of Pharmaceutical Sciences
