# Health Canada — Clarifax Request

**Date:** 19 October 2023
**To:** Borealis Therapeutics Inc.
**Product:** BRX-330 (salvenidol) 100 mcg inhalation powder
**Submission:** NDS Control No. 270455

The following clarification is requested to complete the quality review.

## Q1. Excipient grade

Module 3.2.P.4 identifies the carrier as lactose monohydrate but does not state the grade. Please confirm the grade of lactose monohydrate (including particle size distribution specification) used in the registration and commercial batches, and justify its suitability for inhalation use.

## Q2. Stability under in-use conditions

The in-use stability study supports 6 weeks after removal from the foil pouch at 25°C/75% RH. Please clarify whether the fine particle dose remained within acceptance criteria at the 6-week timepoint for all three batches.

## Sponsor commitment

In the teleconference of 12 October 2023, Borealis Therapeutics committed to provide the lactose monohydrate grade justification and the in-use fine particle dose data within 15 calendar days of receipt of this request. The response will be coordinated by Priya Raman, Associate Director, Regulatory CMC.
