# Stability Protocol STP-330-001 — Registration Batches

**Product:** BRX-330 (salvenidol) 100 mcg inhalation powder in multidose reservoir device
**Protocol date:** 4 March 2021

## Storage conditions and timepoints

- Long-term: 25°C/60% RH — 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 months
- Intermediate: 30°C/65% RH — 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 months
- Accelerated: 40°C/75% RH — 0, 1, 3, 6 months
- In-use: 25°C/75% RH, device removed from foil pouch — 0, 2, 4, 6 weeks

Devices are stored mouthpiece-up and mouthpiece-down at the long-term condition.

## Acceptance criteria

- Delivered dose uniformity: 80–120% of label claim for 9 of 10 doses; none outside 75–125%
- Fine particle dose (< 5 µm): 30–50 mcg
- Assay: 90.0–110.0%
- Total impurities: NMT 1.5%
- Moisture content: NMT 1.0%

## Scope

All three registration batches (Lots S30-R01, S30-R02, S30-R03) in the commercial device and foil pouch are included. No bracketing or matrixing design is applied; all batches are tested at all timepoints.

## Commitments

Stability data will be reported in Module 3.2.P.8.3 at the time of submission and updated during review as further timepoints become available.
