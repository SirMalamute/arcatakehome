# FDA — Approval Letter (Excerpt: Postmarketing Commitments)

**Date:** 22 January 2020
**NDA:** 214587
**Applicant:** Borealis Therapeutics Inc.
**Product:** BRX-101 (velantrine hydrochloride) tablets, 50 mg

This new drug application provides for the use of BRX-101 tablets for the treatment of moderate to severe chronic plaque psoriasis in adults.

## Postmarketing Commitments (Chemistry, Manufacturing and Controls)

We remind you of your postmarketing commitments listed below:

**PMC 3721-1.** Conduct a photostability study per ICH Q1B on the commercial packaging configuration (HDPE bottle with induction seal) and submit the final report.
Final Report Submission: 30 September 2020

**PMC 3721-2.** Re-evaluate the specification limit for the degradation product RRT 0.84 after 12 months of commercial-scale stability data on at least three batches, and submit a prior approval supplement if the limit is changed.
Final Report Submission: 31 December 2021

Submit clinical protocols to your IND for this product. Submit all final reports to this NDA, clearly designated "Postmarketing Commitment Final Report".

Sincerely,

Division of Dermatology and Dentistry
Office of New Drugs, CDER
