# Module 3.2.P.4 — Control of Excipients

**Product:** BRX-330 (salvenidol) 100 mcg inhalation powder
**Document date:** 21 November 2023
**Sequence:** response to Health Canada clarifax of 19 October 2023

## Lactose monohydrate

The carrier is inhalation-grade lactose monohydrate, Respitose SV003, sieved grade. The particle size distribution specification is: d10 15–35 µm, d50 50–70 µm, d90 90–125 µm (laser diffraction), with fine lactose (< 5 µm) not more than 3%.

## Justification

The fine lactose fraction strongly influences fine particle dose. Development studies showed that lots with more than 5% fine lactose increased fine particle dose by approximately 20%, outside the clinical batch range. The PSD limits therefore bracket the lots used in the pivotal clinical batches.

The lactose supplier provides a TSE/BSE certificate of suitability, and the milk used is from healthy animals fit for human consumption, in line with Ph. Eur. 5.2.8.

Guidance applied: EMA Guideline on the Pharmaceutical Quality of Inhalation and Nasal Products (EMEA/CHMP/QWP/49313/2005) and FDA draft guidance "Metered Dose Inhaler (MDI) and Dry Powder Inhaler (DPI) Products — Quality Considerations" (2018).
