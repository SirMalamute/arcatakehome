# Deviation Report DEV-2023-0417

**Date opened:** 17 April 2023
**Product:** BRX-204 (orvatimab) 150 mg/mL prefilled syringe
**Category:** Stability — chamber excursion (major)

## Description

Stability chamber SC-07 (5°C ± 3°C) recorded temperatures of up to 14.2°C for 31 hours over the weekend of 15–16 April 2023 due to a compressor failure. The chamber held the 18-month timepoint samples for lots OV-C21, OV-C22 and OV-C23.

## Impact assessment

The mean kinetic temperature over the excursion was 11.8°C. Supportive data from the 25°C accelerated study show no significant change in high molecular weight species or potency after 1 month. The excursion is therefore assessed as having no impact on the validity of the 18-month results.

## CAPA

CAPA-2023-112: install redundant compressor on SC-07 and add SMS alarms to the on-call rota. Due date: 30 June 2023. Owner: Head of Stability Operations, Raj Patel.

## Status

Closed 12 July 2023 — CAPA completed late (installed 7 July 2023).
