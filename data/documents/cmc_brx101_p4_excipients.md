# Module 3.2.P.4 — Control of Excipients

**Product:** BRX-101 (velantrine hydrochloride) 50 mg film-coated tablets
**Document date:** 18 March 2019
**Sequence:** 0000 (original NDA submission)

## 3.2.P.4.1 Specifications

| Excipient | Function | Grade | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microcrystalline cellulose | Diluent | Avicel PH-102 | USP-NF / Ph. Eur. |
| Croscarmellose sodium | Disintegrant | Ac-Di-Sol | USP-NF / Ph. Eur. |
| Magnesium stearate | Lubricant | Vegetable source, HyQual | USP-NF / Ph. Eur. |
| Hypromellose | Film former | Opadry II 85F (HPMC 2910) | Supplier specification |

## 3.2.P.4.4 Justification of Specifications

Microcrystalline cellulose Avicel PH-102 was selected over PH-101 because its larger mean particle size (approximately 100 µm) provides the flow required for direct compression at commercial press speeds. Development batches manufactured with PH-101 showed tablet weight variability above 3% RSD.

Magnesium stearate of vegetable origin is used to avoid TSE/BSE risk. The specific surface area is controlled at 6–10 m²/g because over-lubrication was shown to slow dissolution in the design-of-experiments study (3.2.P.2.3).

The excipient specifications follow the compendial monographs, and the control strategy is consistent with ICH Q6A and ICH Q8(R2). Excipients are tested on receipt for identity, with full compendial testing at least annually per supplier qualification SOP-QA-014.
