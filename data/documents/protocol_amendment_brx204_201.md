# Protocol Amendment 3 — BRX-204-201

**Title:** A Phase 2b Dose-Ranging Study of Orvatimab in Moderate to Severe Atopic Dermatitis
**Amendment date:** 14 January 2021

## Summary of changes

1. Added a 300 mg every-4-weeks arm following the interim pharmacokinetic analysis.
2. Extended the screening window from 28 to 35 days to accommodate washout of topical calcineurin inhibitors.
3. Clarified that the drug product used from this amendment onward is the 150 mg/mL formulation containing super-refined polysorbate 80, replacing the 100 mg/mL formulation.

## Rationale

The interim PK analysis showed exposure below target at the 150 mg every-4-weeks dose. The formulation change aligns the clinical material with the intended commercial product.

## Regulatory

This amendment was submitted to FDA under IND 145872 and to Health Canada as a Clinical Trial Application amendment.
