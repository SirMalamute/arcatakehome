# Health Canada — Labelling Review Letter

**Date:** 3 May 2022
**To:** Borealis Therapeutics Inc.
**Product:** BRX-204 (orvatimab) 150 mg/mL solution for subcutaneous injection
**Submission:** SNDS Control No. 261974

Dear Sponsor,

The Directorate has reviewed the revised Product Monograph submitted on 22 March 2022.

1. Part I, Section 4.2 (Recommended Dose and Dosage Adjustment): the statement regarding missed doses must be revised to align with the Canadian Reference Product wording.
2. Part III (Patient Medication Information): plain-language descriptions of injection-site reactions must be added.
3. The inner carton label must display the bilingual drug identification number in a font of no less than 8 points.

Borealis Therapeutics committed to submit the revised Product Monograph and carton mock-ups within 30 calendar days of the date of this letter. The Labelling Manager, Marc Tremblay, is responsible for the response.

No quality or clinical deficiencies were identified in this cycle.

Regards,

Office of Submissions and Intellectual Property
Health Canada
