# Health Canada — Notice of Compliance with Conditions Follow-up

**Date:** 14 September 2021
**To:** Regulatory Affairs, Borealis Therapeutics Inc.
**Product:** BRX-101 (velantrine hydrochloride) 50 mg film-coated tablets
**Submission:** NDS Control No. 248311

Dear Regulatory Affairs Team,

The Pharmaceutical Drugs Directorate has completed its review of the information submitted on 2 July 2021 in response to the Screening Deficiency Notice for BRX-101.

## Quality (Chemistry and Manufacturing)

The Directorate notes that the proposed 36-month shelf life is supported by only 18 months of long-term stability data at 25°C/60% RH on three primary batches. A shelf life of 24 months is granted at this time.

Borealis Therapeutics committed to submit the 24-month and 36-month long-term stability results for the three primary batches (Lots BX1-0012, BX1-0013, BX1-0014) by 31 March 2023 as a Level II change.

The Directorate further notes that the dissolution acceptance criterion of Q = 75% at 30 minutes is wider than supported by the clinical batches. The sponsor agreed to tighten the criterion to Q = 80% at 30 minutes once 10 commercial batches have been manufactured, and to report the outcome in the next Annual Drug Notification.

## Responsibility

Please direct correspondence on these commitments to the CMC Regulatory Lead, Dr. Hannah Okafor, who has been designated as the owner of the post-approval stability commitments for BRX-101.

Sincerely,

Bureau of Pharmaceutical Sciences
Health Canada
