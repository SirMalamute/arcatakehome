# Clinical Study Report Synopsis — BRX-101-301

**Title:** A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of BRX-101 in Adults with Moderate to Severe Chronic Plaque Psoriasis
**Report date:** 28 February 2019

## Design

Patients (N = 642) were randomized 2:1 to BRX-101 50 mg once daily or placebo for 16 weeks, followed by an open-label extension to week 52.

## Primary endpoint

PASI 75 response at week 16: 58.4% (BRX-101) vs 11.2% (placebo); difference 47.2% (95% CI 40.9–53.5), p < 0.001.

## Safety

The most common adverse events were nausea (9.8%), headache (7.1%) and upper respiratory tract infection (6.5%). Two patients in the BRX-101 arm discontinued due to elevated ALT above 5× ULN, both resolving after discontinuation.

## Drug product

Clinical supplies were manufactured from Lots BX1-0012 and BX1-0013, which are also primary stability batches.
