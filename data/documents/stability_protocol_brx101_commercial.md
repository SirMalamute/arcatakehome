# Stability Protocol STP-101-004 — Commercial Batches

**Product:** BRX-101 (velantrine hydrochloride) 50 mg film-coated tablets
**Protocol date:** 10 August 2020
**Packaging:** 30-count HDPE bottle with induction seal and desiccant canister

## 1. Purpose

To establish and confirm the shelf life of BRX-101 commercial batches in accordance with ICH Q1A(R2).

## 2. Storage conditions

| Study | Condition | Timepoints (months) |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term | 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH | 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36 |
| Intermediate | 30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH | 0, 6, 9, 12 (tested only if accelerated fails) |
| Accelerated | 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH | 0, 3, 6 |

## 3. Tests and acceptance criteria

Assay 95.0–105.0%; degradation product RRT 0.84 NMT 0.3%; total degradation products NMT 1.0%; dissolution Q = 75% at 30 minutes; water content NMT 4.0%.

## 4. Bracketing and matrixing

Bracketing is applied to bottle count: only the smallest (30-count) and largest (500-count) presentations are tested, as all presentations use the same HDPE resin, closure, and desiccant-to-tablet ratio. The intermediate 90-count presentation is therefore represented by the extremes, as permitted by ICH Q1D.

## 5. Post-approval commitment

The first three commercial batches will be placed on long-term stability, and one batch per year thereafter, as committed in the NDA.

## 6. Deviations

Any excursion from storage conditions greater than 24 hours must be documented as a deviation per SOP-QA-021 and assessed for impact.
