# Module 3.2.P.4 — Control of Excipients (Updated)

**Product:** BRX-101 (velantrine hydrochloride) 50 mg film-coated tablets
**Document date:** 2 February 2024
**Sequence:** 0031 (CBE-30 supplement, alternate diluent supplier)

## Summary of change

An alternate supplier of microcrystalline cellulose has been qualified. The grade is changed from Avicel PH-102 to Vivapur 200 (mean particle size approximately 250 µm), which is now the current specification for all commercial manufacture from Q2 2024.

## 3.2.P.4.1 Specifications (current)

| Excipient | Function | Grade | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microcrystalline cellulose | Diluent | Vivapur 200 | USP-NF / Ph. Eur. |
| Croscarmellose sodium | Disintegrant | Ac-Di-Sol | USP-NF / Ph. Eur. |
| Magnesium stearate | Lubricant | Vegetable source, HyQual | USP-NF / Ph. Eur. |
| Hypromellose | Film former | Opadry II 85F (HPMC 2910) | Supplier specification |

## Justification

Three comparability batches using Vivapur 200 met all release specifications, and dissolution profiles were similar to the reference batches (f2 = 68). The change was assessed under FDA guidance "Changes to an Approved NDA or ANDA" (2004) and SUPAC-IR as a Level 2 excipient change.
