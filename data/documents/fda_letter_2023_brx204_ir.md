# FDA — Information Request

**Date:** 7 February 2023
**BLA:** 761299
**Product:** BRX-204 (orvatimab) injection, 150 mg/mL
**Applicant:** Borealis Therapeutics Inc.

We are reviewing your biologics license application and request the following information.

1. **Container closure integrity.** Provide container closure integrity data (deterministic method, e.g., helium leak or high-voltage leak detection) for the prefilled syringe at the end of the proposed 24-month shelf life at 2–8°C.

2. **Polysorbate 80 degradation.** Provide data on polysorbate 80 content over the stability program, including the analytical method and acceptance criterion. Discuss the risk of particle formation from polysorbate hydrolysis.

During the teleconference on 2 February 2023, you agreed to submit the response to items 1 and 2 by 7 March 2023. You also agreed to add polysorbate 80 content to the release and stability specification as a post-approval commitment, with the updated specification to be submitted in the first annual report.

Please contact the Regulatory Project Manager with questions. Your designated point of contact for this response is Dr. Elena Vasquez, Head of Biologics CMC.

Office of Pharmaceutical Quality, CDER
