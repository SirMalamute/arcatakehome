# Module 3.2.P.4 — Control of Excipients

**Product:** BRX-204 (orvatimab) 150 mg/mL solution for subcutaneous injection
**Document date:** 6 September 2022

| Excipient | Function | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| L-Histidine / L-histidine HCl | Buffer | Multi-compendial, low endotoxin |
| Sucrose | Stabilizer / tonicity | High purity, low endotoxin (USP-NF/Ph. Eur./JP) |
| Polysorbate 80 | Surfactant | Super-refined, low peroxide (< 5 mEq/kg) |
| Water for injection | Solvent | Ph. Eur. / USP |

## Justification

Super-refined polysorbate 80 with a peroxide value below 5 mEq/kg was selected because oxidation of methionine residues in the Fc region correlated with peroxide content in forced degradation studies. Standard-grade polysorbate 80 lots with peroxide values above 10 mEq/kg produced a 3-fold increase in oxidized species after 3 months at 25°C.

Low-endotoxin grades of sucrose and histidine are used because the product is administered parenterally and the endotoxin limit of the drug product is 0.5 EU/mg.

Excipient selection and control follow ICH Q8(R2), ICH Q6B, and the EMA Guideline on Excipients in the Dossier for Application for Marketing Authorisation of a Medicinal Product (EMEA/CHMP/QWP/396951/2006).
