# SOP-QA-021 — Management of Stability Chamber Excursions

**Effective date:** 1 June 2019
**Owner:** Quality Assurance, Stability Operations

## 1. Scope

This SOP applies to all ICH stability chambers and walk-in rooms used for registration, commercial, and post-approval stability studies.

## 2. Definitions

An excursion is any period in which chamber temperature or relative humidity is outside the qualified range (±2°C, ±5% RH) for more than 60 consecutive minutes.

## 3. Procedure

3.1 The chamber monitoring system alarms the on-call stability scientist within 15 minutes of an excursion.
3.2 Excursions longer than 24 hours must be documented as a deviation in the quality management system within 2 business days.
3.3 The stability lead performs an impact assessment using mean kinetic temperature (MKT) calculations per USP <1079>.
3.4 Samples may be relocated to a qualified backup chamber with QA approval.

## 4. Responsibilities

The Head of Stability Operations is accountable for the timely closure of excursion deviations within 30 days.
